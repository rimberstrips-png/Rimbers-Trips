# Rimbers Business Hub

Rimbers Trip — Corporate / Business Listing System

Add a dedicated Corporate Sign Up system to Rimbers Trip for businesses that want to list their properties, services, and travel offerings on the platform.

Corporate Sign Up

Add a prominent “List Your Business” or “For Businesses” option in the main navigation.

Corporate users should be able to register as:

Hotel

Resort

Apartment

Villa

Guest House

Hostel

Travel Agency

Tour Operator

Car Rental Company

Experience Provider

Other Travel Business

Corporate Registration

Create a professional multi-step registration process.

Step 1 — Business Account

Collect:

Business name

Business email

Phone number

Password

Country

Business type

Step 2 — Business Information

Collect:

Legal business name

Business display name

Business category

Business description

Business registration/license number

Tax information

Website

Business phone

Business email

Step 3 — Business Location

Allow the business to enter:

Country

City

Address

Postal code

Google/Map location

Latitude/longitude

Show the location on an interactive map.

Step 4 — Verification

Allow businesses to upload relevant documents such as:

Business registration certificate

License

Tax documents

Identity/ownership documents

Other required verification documents

Show the verification status:

Pending → Under Review → Verified / Rejected

Business Listing Setup

After registration, guide the corporate user through creating their first listing.

For a hotel/property, allow them to add:

Property name

Property type

Description

Photos

Location

Amenities

Check-in/check-out times

Property policies

Cancellation policy

Contact information

Facilities

Rooms

Allow businesses to create multiple room types.

Each room can have:

Room name

Photos

Description

Bed type

Number of guests

Number of rooms

Amenities

Price per night

Weekend pricing

Seasonal pricing

Availability

Cancellation policy

Breakfast options

Business Dashboard

Create a dedicated Corporate Dashboard separate from the customer dashboard.

Dashboard Overview

Display:

Total bookings

Upcoming bookings

Today's check-ins

Today's check-outs

Revenue

Pending payouts

Property views

Conversion rate

Customer reviews

Property Management

Businesses can:

Add property

Edit property

Upload photos

Manage amenities

Manage rooms

Update prices

Manage availability

Temporarily disable listing

Delete listing

Booking Management

Show:

Upcoming bookings

Current stays

Completed bookings

Cancelled bookings

Each booking should display:

Booking ID

Guest name

Property/room

Check-in

Check-out

Guests

Amount

Payment status

Booking status

Provide actions such as:

View booking

Accept/confirm where applicable

Cancel

Contact guest

Download invoice

Availability & Pricing

Provide a calendar-based management system where businesses can:

Open/close dates

Set room availability

Change prices

Add seasonal prices

Add weekend prices

Create promotional rates

Block rooms

Manage minimum stay requirements

Business Analytics

Provide charts and statistics for:

Revenue

Bookings

Occupancy

Property views

Conversion rate

Average booking value

Popular room types

Customer ratings

Monthly performance

Reviews

Businesses can view customer reviews and ratings.

Display:

Overall rating

Category ratings

Customer comments

Review date

Allow businesses to respond professionally to customer reviews.

Payments & Payouts

Create a financial section showing:

Total earnings

Pending payouts

Completed payouts

Booking revenue

Platform fees

Refunds

Transaction history

Allow businesses to configure their payout information.

Business Profile

Create a public-facing business profile containing:

Business logo

Cover image

Business name

Verification badge

Description

Location

Contact information

Listed properties/services

Reviews

Ratings

Listing Status

Every business listing should have a clear status:

Draft

Pending Review

Published

Suspended

Rejected

Only approved and verified listings should appear publicly on Rimbers Trip.

Corporate Navigation

For business users, provide a dedicated navigation structure:

Dashboard | Properties | Bookings | Calendar | Pricing | Reviews | Analytics | Payments | Business Profile | Settings

The corporate experience should feel like a professional property/business management platform, while the customer side should remain focused on discovering and booking trips.

The overall Rimbers Trip ecosystem should therefore have three clear experiences:

Customer — Discover and book travel

Corporate/Business — List and manage businesses

Admin — Manage and control the entire Rimbers Trip marketplace, take the coperate login idea from screenshots, make Full UI of it
